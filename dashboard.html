Marketing Analytics Dashboard

Part 1: GA4 funnel and channel analysis (real data: Google Merchandise Store, Nov 2020 to Jan 2021)

  • Channels: Referral converts best (1.66% of sessions, $1.31/session); Paid Search converts worst (0.98%, $0.58/session).
  • Funnel: only 20% of users who view a product add it to the cart, and 46% of those who begin checkout complete the purchase (54% abandon). Overall, 1.66% of visitors buy.
  • Device: mobile converts at 1.71% vs 1.62% on desktop, so the leaks are in the product page and checkout flow on every device, not a mobile-specific problem.
  • Seasonality: revenue peaked on Nov 30, 2020 ($11,990, Cyber Monday). Through Jan 25 the store earned $359,972 from 5,410 purchases.
  • Landing pages (5,000+ sessions): / converts best (1.77%). /google+redesign/apparel/google+dino+game+tee brings 18,445 sessions but converts at 0.02% with 45% engagement, the clearest page to fix.

Part 2: Paid campaign performance (simulated data: 10,000 campaign rows, 2024)

  • Traffic vs conversion: Google Ads drove the most traffic (360,311 sessions) but converted at 4.50%, well below Email (7.75%).
  • Bounce: Instagram has the highest bounce rate (65.2%), pointing to a landing page or targeting problem for that traffic.
  • Paid efficiency: Google Ads returns the most per ad dollar and Facebook the least (ROAS 215x vs 67x).

The GA4 sample has no ad spend, so ROAS comes from this simulated dataset. Its spend values are synthetic and unrealistically low, so compare ROAS across channels rather than reading the absolute numbers.
